Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate?

Any teenager 18 years old or younger anywhere on earth can join. Whether you attend school, study at home, or code in your free time, you are welcome.

Do I need experience?

No. Beginners are welcome. If this is your very first website, you will find friendly peers in Slack ready to share templates and answer questions whenever you get stuck.

Does it cost anything?

No. Taking part is completely free of charge. You build, you ship, and Hack Club provides the ticket grant.

What counts as a valid project?

A live website clearly themed around a movie, built by you, with a public repo. It can feature custom interactive scene recreations, custom soundtracks, trivia games, or artistic tributes.

How are hours counted and how do I get my grant?

Install the Hackatime plugin in VS Code or your preferred editor to track time while programming. When you ship, submit your repository and live address. After verification, grants are disbursed via prepaid debit card or direct cinema reimbursement.

Can I use AI tools?

You may consult AI tools as a learning assistant to explain concepts or debug errors. However, the core design and code must be authored by your own hands. Auto generated websites created by a single prompt do not qualify.

When does it start and end?

Tickets haven't started yet, and we are planning on launching soon. Check our Slack channel for the final curtain call announcement.

Who runs this?

Organized by teen makers in the Hack Club community with funding and support provided by Hack Club.
